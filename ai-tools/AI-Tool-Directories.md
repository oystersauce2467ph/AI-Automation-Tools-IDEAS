# AI Tool Directories, Rankings & Datasets

Websites and resources that catalog, rank, and classify AI tools. Use these to keep the
[master list](./AI-Tools-Master.csv) fresh and to discover new tools by category.
*Content was rephrased for compliance with licensing restrictions.*

## 🏆 Traffic & Usage Rankings (best for "most-used" data)

| Source | What it offers | Link |
|--------|----------------|------|
| RankmyAI | Monthly Top 100 by traffic + overall score, from a 62,000+ tool database; includes category, pricing, and country | [rankmyai.com](https://www.rankmyai.com/rankings/top-100-ai-tools-by-traffic) |
| Exploding Topics | 65 most popular AI tools ranked by monthly visits, plus fastest-growing tools | [explodingtopics.com](https://explodingtopics.com/blog/most-popular-ai-tools) |
| a16z (Andreessen Horowitz) | "Top 100 Gen AI Consumer Apps" report (web + mobile), published ~twice a year | [a16z.com](https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-6/) |
| Similarweb | Underlying web-traffic analytics many rankings cite | [similarweb.com](https://www.similarweb.com/) |
| One Little Web | Segment rankings, e.g. top AI business tools by traffic/market share | [onelittleweb.com](https://onelittleweb.com/digital-market-intelligence/ai-tools/business/) |
| Momentic | Market-share tracking for top AI chatbots/LLMs | [momenticmarketing.com](https://momenticmarketing.com/blog/top-ai-chatbots) |

### How to Refresh the Top 100
1. Pull the latest monthly ranking from **RankmyAI** and **Exploding Topics** (traffic).
2. Cross-check consumer momentum against the newest **a16z** report.
3. Fill category gaps using **There's An AI For That** / **Futurepedia**.
4. Update [`AI-Tools-Master.csv`](./AI-Tools-Master.csv) and regenerate [`Filtered-Lists.md`](./Filtered-Lists.md).
