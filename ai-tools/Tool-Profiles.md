# AI Tool Deep Profiles — Batch 1 (Flagship Tools)

Detailed profiles for the most-used AI tools: release date, pricing (free tier + paid plans), a short

## 🤖 Chatbots & Assistants

### ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- **Released:** November 30, 2022
- **Pricing:** Free (with limits) · Go $8 · Plus $20 · Pro $100 · Pro Max $200 · Business $25/seat · Enterprise (custom). No time-limited trial; the free tier is permanent.
- **Description:** The most-used general AI assistant — writing, coding, analysis, images, and voice in one app.
- **Main features:** GPT-5-class reasoning, voice mode, image generation, file/data analysis, web browsing, custom GPTs, memory across chats.
- **Hidden features:** Build no-code **custom GPTs** with Actions that call external APIs; **Projects** to group chats with shared files/instructions; use **"canvas"** for side-by-side editing of docs/code; scheduled **Tasks** that run and message you later.

### Google Gemini
- **Released:** March 2023 (as Bard); rebranded Gemini February 2024
- **Pricing:** Free · Google AI Plus $7.99 · AI Pro $19.99 · AI Ultra $99.99 (Ultra was cut from ~$250).
- **Description:** Google's multimodal assistant, deeply tied into Search, Workspace, and Android.
- **Main features:** Long-context reasoning, image/video generation (Veo/Flow), Gmail/Docs/Drive integration, Deep Research, Gems (custom experts).
- **Hidden features:** **Gems** let you save reusable custom assistants; **@-mention Google apps** to pull live data; **Audio Overviews** via NotebookLM tie-in; huge context window for whole-document analysis.

### Claude (Anthropic)
- **Released:** March 2023
- **Pricing:** Free · Pro $20 ($17 annual) · Max 5x $100 · Max 20x $200 · Team $25/seat · Enterprise (custom).
- **Description:** Safety-focused assistant known for long-context reasoning, writing quality, and coding.
- **Main features:** Very large context window, Projects, Artifacts (live previews), Claude Code, computer-use/agentic workflows, low hallucination rates.
- **Hidden features:** **Artifacts** render live apps/diagrams/documents beside the chat and can be published/shared; **Projects** add persistent knowledge; **Styles** teach Claude your writing voice; **MCP** connectors plug Claude into your own tools.

### Grok (xAI)
- **Released:** November 2023
- **Pricing:** Free (limited) · SuperGrok ~$30 · also via X Premium+ (~$40).
- **Description:** Conversational AI built into X with real-time access to public posts.
- **Main features:** Live web/X search, image generation, "think" reasoning mode, coding help.
- **Hidden features:** Real-time trend awareness from X; **DeepSearch** for sourced research; a less-filtered "fun" personality mode.

### Microsoft Copilot
- **Released:** February 2023 (as Bing Chat)
- **Pricing:** Free · Copilot Pro $20 · Microsoft 365 Copilot $30/seat.
- **Description:** Microsoft's assistant across Windows, Edge, and the Office apps.
- **Main features:** Document/email drafting in Word/Outlook, Excel analysis, Teams meeting recaps, image creation, web grounding.
- **Hidden features:** **Copilot Vision** can see and guide you through your screen; **Copilot Pages** turn chats into a shared canvas; deep **Excel** formula/data automation many users never try.

## 🔎 Search & Answer Engines

### Perplexity AI
- **Released:** December 2022
- **Pricing:** Free · Pro $20 ($200/yr) · Max $200 · Education Pro $10 (students) · Enterprise $40/seat.
- **Description:** An "answer engine" that responds with cited, real-time web sources.

## ✍️ Writing & Grammar

### Grammarly
- **Released:** 2015 (writing assistant)
- **Pricing:** Free · Pro $12 (annual) · Business $15/seat.
- **Description:** Real-time grammar, clarity, tone, and rewriting assistant that works across the web.
- **Main features:** Grammar/spelling, tone detection, full-sentence rewrites, plagiarism check, generative AI prompts.
- **Hidden features:** **Custom style guides & snippets** (Business); tone adjustment per audience; works inside almost any desktop app, not just the browser.

### QuillBot
- **Released:** 2017
- **Pricing:** Free · Premium ~$10 (annual).
- **Description:** Paraphrasing-first writing tool with grammar and summarization.
- **Main features:** Paraphraser with modes, summarizer, grammar checker, citation generator, AI detector.
- **Hidden features:** Adjustable **synonym slider** to control how much text changes; built-in **translator** and **flow** rewriting for whole paragraphs.

## 🎨 Image Generation

### Midjourney
- **Released:** July 2022
- **Pricing:** No free tier · Basic $10 · Standard $30 · Pro $60 · Mega $120 (20% off annual).
- **Description:** Premium text-to-image generator prized for artistic quality.
- **Main features:** High-fidelity image generation, style references, character consistency, upscaling, web + Discord.
- **Hidden features:** **--sref** (style reference) and **--cref** (character reference) codes for consistency; **Relax mode** for unlimited slow generations on Standard+; **Stealth mode** (Pro+) keeps images private.

### Leonardo AI
- **Released:** December 2022
- **Pricing:** Free (daily tokens) · Apprentice ~$12 · Artisan ~$30 · Maestro ~$60.
- **Description:** Image generator popular for game art and design assets.
- **Main features:** Fine-tuned models, image guidance/ControlNet, real-time canvas, 3D texture generation.
- **Hidden features:** **Elements** to blend trained styles; **Live Canvas** sketch-to-image; token top-ups without upgrading tiers.

## 🎬 Video

### CapCut
- **Released:** 2020
- **Pricing:** Free · Pro ~$10.
- **Description:** Massively popular mobile/desktop editor with AI baked into its best features.
- **Main features:** Auto-captions, background removal, text-to-speech, AI effects, templates, text-to-video.
- **Hidden features:** **Auto-reframe** for multi-platform aspect ratios; **script-to-video**; long-video-to-Shorts; huge free template library that auto-syncs edits.

### Runway
- **Released:** 2018 (Gen video models from 2023)
- **Pricing:** Free (credits) · Standard $15 · Pro $35 · Unlimited $95.
- **Description:** Generative video/creative suite used by filmmakers and marketers.
- **Main features:** Text/image-to-video, video-to-video, motion brush, green-screen, frame interpolation.
- **Hidden features:** **Motion Brush** to animate specific regions; **Director Mode** camera controls; **Act-One** to drive characters from your own performance.

### HeyGen
- **Released:** 2020
- **Pricing:** Free (1 min/day) · Creator ~$29 · Team ~$39/seat.
- **Description:** AI avatar and video-translation platform for talking-head content at scale.
- **Main features:** Realistic avatars, script-to-video, 175+ language translation with lip-sync, brand kits.
- **Hidden features:** **Instant Avatar** clones you from a short webcam clip; **interactive avatars** for real-time use; API for bulk personalized videos.

## 🔊 Audio, Music & Voice

## 💻 Coding & Development

### GitHub Copilot
- **Released:** June 2021 (GA June 2022)
- **Pricing:** Free (limited) · Pro $10 · Pro+ $39 · Business $19/seat · Enterprise $39/seat.
- **Description:** The original AI pair-programmer, embedded in major IDEs.
- **Main features:** Inline completions, chat, agent mode, multi-file edits, PR summaries, model choice.
- **Hidden features:** **/slash commands** and `@workspace` context in chat; **Copilot in the CLI**; custom instructions per repo; knowledge bases (Enterprise).

### Cursor
- **Released:** 2023
- **Pricing:** Free (Hobby) · Pro $20 · Pro+ $60 · Ultra $200 · Teams $40/seat (annual ~20% off).
- **Description:** An AI-native fork of VS Code built around agentic coding.
- **Main features:** Tab completions, Agent mode, codebase-wide chat, multi-file edits, MCP integrations.
- **Hidden features:** **`.cursorrules`** to encode project conventions; **@Docs** to index external documentation; **Background/Cloud Agents** for parallel tasks.

### Replit
- **Released:** 2016 (AI Agent later)
- **Pricing:** Free (Starter) · Core $20 · Teams $35/seat.
- **Description:** Browser IDE where an AI agent builds and deploys full apps.
- **Main features:** Agent app-building, instant hosting/deploys, collaborative editing, 50+ languages, mobile app.
- **Hidden features:** **Agent** can build and deploy from a prompt end-to-end; built-in **databases/secrets/auth**; one-click custom domains.

## 🗂️ Productivity & Design

### Notion AI
- **Released:** February 2023
- **Pricing:** Free (limited AI) · Plus $10 · Business $20/seat (AI included).
- **Description:** AI woven into the popular all-in-one workspace for docs, wikis, and projects.
- **Main features:** AI writing/editing, Q&A across your workspace, connected search (Slack/Drive), autofill database properties.
- **Hidden features:** **AI Autofill** to summarize/tag database rows automatically; **Enterprise Search** across connected apps; AI meeting-note transcription.

### Canva (Magic Studio)
- **Released:** 2023 (Canva since 2013)
- **Pricing:** Free · Pro $15 · Teams $10/seat · Enterprise (custom).
- **Description:** The mass-market design suite with a full set of AI "Magic" tools.
- **Main features:** Magic Design, text-to-image, Magic Write, background remover, Magic Switch (repurpose to other formats), video editing.
- **Hidden features:** **Magic Switch** converts one design into blogs/slides/social posts; **Brand Kit** auto-applies fonts/colors; **Bulk Create** generates many variations from a dataset.

### Gamma
- **Released:** 2022
- **Pricing:** Free (credits) · Plus ~$10 · Pro ~$20.
- **Description:** Generates polished presentations, docs, and websites from a prompt or outline.
- **Main features:** Prompt-to-deck, AI restyle, one-click themes, analytics, publish-to-web.
- **Hidden features:** Paste an outline/doc to **import-and-format** instantly; **card-based** editing that stays responsive; built-in **analytics** on shared decks.

## ⚙️ Automation & Translation

### Zapier
- **Released:** 2011 (AI features from 2023)
- **Pricing:** Free (100 tasks/mo) · Pro ~$30 · Team ~$104.
- **Description:** Connects 6,000+ apps to automate workflows — now with AI agents and steps.
- **Main features:** Multi-step Zaps, AI-by-Zapier steps, Agents, Tables, Interfaces, Chatbots.
- **Hidden features:** **AI steps** to parse/format data mid-workflow without code; **Agents** that act across your connected apps; **Paths** for conditional branching.

### DeepL
- **Released:** 2017
- **Pricing:** Free · Starter ~$9 · Advanced ~$29 · Pro (custom).
- **Description:** Translation service widely rated more natural than the alternatives.
- **Main features:** 30+ languages, document translation, DeepL Write (style/grammar), glossary, API.
- **Hidden features:** **Glossaries** enforce preferred terminology; **DeepL Write** improves tone/phrasing in your own language; whole-document translation preserves formatting.
